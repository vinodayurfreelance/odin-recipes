Paneer Butter Masala

A rich and creamy Indian dish made with paneer (cottage cheese) in a spiced tomato-based sauce. It's a popular comfort food that's perfect for special occasions or a cozy family dinner.

Preparation Time: 20 minutes

Cooking Time: 30 minutes

Total Time: 50 minutes

Servings: 4 servings

Course: Main Course

Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients:

  • 200g paneer, cubed
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander leaves for garnish

Instructions:

  1. Cube the paneer and set aside.
  2. Heat butter in a pan over medium heat.
  3. Add cumin seeds and let them splutter.
  4. Add chopped onions and sauté until golden brown.
  5. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for another minute.
  6. Add chopped tomatoes and cook until they turn soft and mushy.
