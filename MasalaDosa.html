Masala Dosa

A crispy and delicious South Indian breakfast dish made with a thin crepe filled with a spicy potato filling and served with coconut chutney.

Preparation Time: 20 minutes

Cooking Time: 15 minutes

Total Time: 35 minutes

Servings: 4 servings

Course: Breakfast

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup urad dal (black gram), soaked for 2 hours
  • 1 cup idli rice, soaked for 2 hours
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 tablespoon oil or ghee
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 2 potatoes, boiled and mashed
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander leaves for garnish

Instructions:

  1. Grind the soaked urad dal and idli rice separately to a smooth paste, adding water as needed.
  2. Mix the two pastes together and add salt. Let it rest for 30 minutes.
  3. Heat oil or ghee in a pan over medium heat. Pour a ladle of batter and spread it evenly.
