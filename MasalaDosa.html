Masala Dosa

A crispy and delicious South Indian breakfast dish made with a thin crepe filled with a spicy potato filling and served with coconut chutney.

  • Preparation Time: 20 minutes
  • Cooking Time: 15 minutes
  • Total Time: 35 minutes
  • Servings: 4 servings
  • Course: Breakfast
  • Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup rice (preferably parboiled rice)
  • 1/4 cup urad dal (split black gram)
  • 1/4 teaspoon fenugreek seeds
  • Salt to taste
  • Water for soaking and grinding
  • Oil or ghee for cooking the dosa
  • For the potato filling:
  • 3 medium potatoes, boiled and mashed
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 green chilies, finely chopped
  • 1 teaspoon mustard seeds
  • A pinch of turmeric powder
  • A few curry leaves
  • Salt to taste
  • Coriander leaves for garnish

Instructions:

  1. Grind the soaked urad dal and idli rice separately to a smooth paste, adding water as needed.
  2. Mix the two pastes together and add salt. Let it rest for 30 minutes.
  3. Heat oil or ghee in a pan over medium heat. Pour a ladle of batter and spread it evenly.
