Chana Masala

A hearty and flavorful North Indian dish made with chickpeas cooked in a spiced tomato-based sauce. It's a protein-packed vegetarian option that pairs well with rice or flatbreads like roti or naan.

  • Preparation Time: 15 minutes
  • Cooking Time: 20 minutes
  • Total Time: 35 minutes
  • Servings: 4 servings
  • Course: Breakfast / Snack
  • Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients:

  • 2 cup chickpeas, soake overnight
  • 2 tsp vegetable oil
  • 1 large onion,finely chopped
  • 2 tomatos,finely chopped
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1-2 green chilies,finely chopped
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander leaves for garnish

Instructions:

  1. Rinse and drain the cooked chickpeas and set them aside
  2. Heat oil in a pan over medium heat.Add cumin seeds and let them splutter.
  3. Add the chopped onions and saute until they turn golden brown.
  4. Add the ginger-garlic paste and green chilies .saute for about one minute.
  5. Add the chopped tomatoes and cooked until theybecome soft and the oil begins to seperate.
  6. Add turmeric powder,red chili powder,coriander powder,garam masala,and salt.Mix well.
  7. Add the cooked chickpeas and stir until they are well coated with the masala.
  8. Add water and bring the mixture to a boil.
  9. Reduce the heat and simmer for 10-15 minutes,stirring occasionally.
  10. light mash a few chickpeas with the back of a spoon to thicken the gravy
  11. Turn off the heat and add lemon juice.
  12. Garnish with fresh coriander leaves and serve hot with roti or nan.
