Chana Masala

A hearty and flavorful North Indian dish made with chickpeas cooked in a spiced tomato-based sauce. It's a protein-packed vegetarian option that pairs well with rice or flatbreads like roti or naan.

Preparation Time: 15 minutes

Cooking Time: 20 minutes

Total Time: 35 minutes

Servings: 4 servings

Course: Breakfast / Snack

Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup rice (preferably parboiled rice)
  • 1/4 cup urad dal (split black gram)
  • 1/4 teaspoon fenugreek seeds
  • Salt to taste
  • Water for soaking and grinding
  • Oil or ghee for cooking the dosa
  • For the potato filling:
  • 3 medium potatoes, boiled and mashed
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 2 green chilies, finely chopped
  • 1 teaspoon mustard seeds
  • A pinch of turmeric powder
  • A few curry leaves
  • Salt to taste
  • Coriander leaves for garnish

Instructions:

  1. Rinse the rice and urad dal separately and soak them in water for at least 4-6 hours or overnight.
  2. Drain the soaked rice and urad dal. Grind them separately to a smooth batter, adding water as needed. Combine both batters in a large bowl, add salt, and mix well. Cover and let it ferment in a warm place for 8-12 hours.
  3. For the potato filling, heat oil in a pan, add mustard seeds, curry leaves, and chopped onions. Sauté until onions are translucent. Add green chilies, turmeric powder, and mashed potatoes. Mix well and cook for a few minutes. Season with salt and garnish with coriander leaves.
  4. Heat a non-stick pan or griddle over medium heat. Pour a ladleful of dosa batter onto the pan and spread it in a circular motion to form a thin crepe. Drizzle some oil or ghee around the edges.
  5. Cook until the bottom is golden brown and crispy. Place a portion of the potato filling on one side of the dosa and fold it over.
  6. Serve hot with coconut chutney and sambar.
